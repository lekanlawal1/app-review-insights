How it works

1Real reviewsYour browser loads up to 500 of the app's most recent reviews straight from Apple.
2Numbers without AIRatings, trends and versions are counted directly, so they're exact.
3AI suggests themesGoogle's Gemini groups the reviews and picks quotes. It never produces a number.
4Code checks the AIEvery quote must appear word for word in the review it cites, and every theme is counted from the reviews themselves.

Apple publishes up to 500 recent reviews per app and country, so this covers recent months, not an app's whole history. Reviews are public; nothing you search is stored, and AI answers for popular apps are reused for a day.

Reviews: Apple App Store customer reviews feed. Themes: Google Gemini, checked in your browser. Built by Lekan Lawal.
